Figure S1. Optimal Calling Region vs. Empirical Timeout Calls

NHL regular-season games, 2022–23 through 2024–25 (n = 468 calls)

How to read. Background heatmap shows the leverage of a timeout call over (game minute, score differential) — leverage is the marginal expected win-probability gain from a successful tactical pause. Red dots are the 468 timeout calls actually made by NHL coaches in 2022–25. The dashed blue line is the model-implied first optimal call time for trailing-by-one (d=−1) and tied (d=0) games at the selected μ. The wedge between empirical clusters (often before minute 50) and the optimal threshold (typically minute 47–58) is the under-use result quantified in the main text.
